    Why this exists

    Capability is the binding constraint.

    Every governance reform we have studied — without exception — has been bottlenecked at some point on capability. Not money, not technology, not even political will. Capability.

    The Learning Hub is our small contribution to that bottleneck. It is free, practitioner-led, and designed around the actual decisions an officer or activist will face — not the ones a textbook expects them to face.

    Self-paced modules

    Short courses you can do on a Saturday morning

    Each module is structured as 4–6 short videos, a downloadable reader, and a self-check exercise. No fees. No certifications. Just better practice.

    Mentorship

    Mentorship by peer officers and senior practitioners

    The Mentorship Programme matches a mentee — a junior officer, a fellow, an intern, or a civil-society practitioner — with a senior who has done the work. Six conversations, six weeks, real momentum.

    Mentors are serving and retired officers, programme leads, and senior academics. The matching is by topic, geography and stage of career.

    • Six 1:1 conversations over six weeks
    • Structured around the mentee's actual work, not generic curriculum
    • Confidential, free, peer-driven
    • Open to officers, fellows, interns and civil-society practitioners
