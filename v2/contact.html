    How we respond

    Honest expectations on response time.

    We are a small platform run with practitioner support. Citizen queries usually get a response within 2–3 working days. Officer / researcher requests, depending on complexity, may take 5–10 days.

    If your question is urgent or sensitive, mark it clearly in the subject — we'll route it to the most relevant collaborator.

    Write to us
